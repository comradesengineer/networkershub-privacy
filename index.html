Privacy Policy - Networkers Hub

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Networkers Hub is an offline-first network toolkit for Android. We do not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising network, or backend server for this app. Almost everything happens on your device. This policy explains the small amount of data that leaves the device and why.

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following in app-private storage on your phone:

  • Saved host profiles: hostnames/IP addresses, ports, usernames, and passwords or private keys you choose to save.
  • SSH keys you generate or import, and their optional passphrases.
  • Command history from terminal sessions.
  • SNMP device profiles, thresholds, tile layouts, and a capped event log.
  • Preferences such as terminal theme, font size, split view, and the biometric-lock setting.

Android backups are disabled for this app (android:allowBackup="false"), so this data is not copied to Google Drive or another device by the operating system. We do not transmit it to any server of ours because we do not have one. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is no account to delete.

2. Data transmitted from your device

Data leaves the device only in these situations:

  • Hosts you configure: when you connect over SSH, Telnet, SFTP or FTP, or poll an SNMP device, the address, credentials, commands and files you enter are sent only to the host you specified. We cannot see this traffic.
  • DNS lookups: domain names you look up are sent to a DNS resolver (by default 8.8.8.8, or a resolver you enter yourself).
  • WHOIS lookups: the domain you enter is sent to whois.iana.org and, where applicable, the registrar's WHOIS server over port 43.
  • Public IP and network info: the app requests your public IP from api.ipify.org / api6.ipify.org and approximate network/ISP information from ipwho.is. These services see your IP address and standard connection metadata.
  • Speed tests: test traffic goes to speed.cloudflare.com. Cloudflare sees your IP address and transfer statistics.
  • Device discovery: LAN scanning sends probing packets to addresses on your local subnet and listens for mDNS/SSDP responses. It also queries SMB/NetBIOS-style name information on your local network.

We do not collect, receive, store, or share any of this information ourselves. These third-party services process requests under their own privacy policies.

3. Permissions used

  • Internet / Network state / Wi-Fi state: required for all network tools.
  • Change Wi-Fi state / Wi-Fi multicast: allows mDNS and SSDP discovery.
  • Fine location: Android 10+ requires this permission to read the SSID/BSSID needed for Wi-Fi and LAN features. The app does not use it to track your location.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices: used only for nearby network details; declared as "never for location" on Android 13+.
  • Notifications and foreground services: so file servers, SNMP monitoring, and live SSH sessions can keep running in the background.
  • Biometric: optional app lock protecting saved hosts and keys.
  • Wake lock / battery optimization: keeps long-running transfers and monitors from being interrupted.

4. Security

You can enable a biometric or device-credential lock in the SSH workspace settings. Saved credentials stay in app-private storage and are never uploaded by us. Because this is a powerful administrative toolkit, use it only on networks and devices you own or are authorized to test.

5. Children

Networkers Hub is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL.
